Method

How scoring works

  • Estimates cash from published flat / intro rates on your one-off + monthly spend.
  • Adds signup cash only if the promo is active, spend clears the hurdle, and known issuer eligibility permits it.
  • Boosts high fuss-free scores; penalises min-spend category cards unless optimizer mode is on.
  • Excludes declared new-to-issuer value when your selected wallet or current/recent issuer history includes that bank; other overlaps retain a soft penalty.
  • Unlisted and cancelled principal cards can be marked by issuer so the 6/12-month lookback is decided in the tool.
  • Does not model FX fees, 0% instalments, or insurance perks — keep those separate.
